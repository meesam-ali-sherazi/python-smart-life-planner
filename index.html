THE PROJECT VISION

One application. Two important parts of everyday life.

Smart Life Planner is a Python-based academic project combining a Pakistan Trip Planner and a Monthly Budget Manager. It demonstrates object-oriented programming, data persistence, calculations, validation, testing and PDF report generation.
